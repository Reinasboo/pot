# POT submission assets

- `pot-product-film.mp4`: narrated 90-second product introduction, 1920×1080 at 60fps.
- `pot-product-film.srt`: matching captions; captions are also visible in the film.
- `cover.png`: film cover.
- `pot-product-screenshots.zip`: six edited 1920×1080 product screenshots.
- `screenshots/`: individual PNG exports.
- `screenshots-contact-sheet.png`: screenshot overview.
- `contact-sheet.png`: ten film scenes at their reading holds.
- `film-stills/`: individual scene stills.

Editable film and screenshot compositions, real UI captures, narration, score, fonts, and
the dependency lockfile are in [apps/film](../apps/film/README.md). The film introduces the
product, explains the money rules, and presents a clearly labelled future/token concept.

The proposed token rationale is in [TOKEN_VISION.md](../docs/TOKEN_VISION.md).
