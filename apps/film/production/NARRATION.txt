Every group has that one plan. The weekend away. The birthday surprise. The idea everyone loves, until someone has to organise the money.

Meet POT. One shared pot for a shared goal. Good things, pooled.

Start with a name, a target, and a deadline. Choose the money rules before anyone chips in. Then share one clear plan with your people.

Contributions use USDC on Base. Funding progress follows confirmed transactions. When the goal is reached, the organiser can withdraw. If a refundable goal is missed, contributors can claim their money back. Crowdfunding pots make the payout wallet and withdrawal timing clear from the start.

But the opportunity is bigger than one collection. Imagine creator communities, event organisers, and other apps using POT as their shared payments infrastructure.

A proposed POT token could help the community govern that network: allocate grants, sponsor transaction fees, and reward useful contributions from organisers and builders. The principle is simple: reward value created, not money moved. USDC remains the money in the pot. The token supports coordination around it.

From one birthday gift to a network of shared possibilities.

Big plans. One shared pot. POT.
