# POT product film

- Audience: hackathon judges and people organizing shared goals.
- Deliverables: one narrated 90-second 1920×1080 60fps film, cover, contact sheet,
  six professionally composed product screenshots, editable Remotion project.
- Primary action demonstrated: choose a group-gift template, enter the plan, and preview its
  actual settlement rules. Transaction concepts are explained with original motion diagrams,
  not fabricated receipts, account histories, or confirmation screens.
- Opening: **Big plans. One shared pot.**
- Brand line: **Good things, pooled.**
- Closing destination: potonbase.vercel.app, the real project URL recorded in the repository.
- User explicitly requests that the film not discuss contract deployment status. The film is a
  product introduction, with no deployment-status segment and no invented live activity.
- Potential and token sections use conditional wording and are labelled future vision / concept.
- Token is not part of the implemented MVP, and no token contract or issuance is implied.

## Creative directions

1. Brand story: large confident type and human group-money situations, on paper and charcoal.
   Typography carries the opening, with a shared vessel connecting scenes.
2. Product stage (chosen): real product screenshots, readable close-ups, a restrained pointer,
   and action-driven transitions. The final third opens into a larger network and token concept.
3. Future pitch: network diagrams and protocol language lead, with product as evidence.
   Less appropriate because judges first need to understand the consumer job.

## Shared style

Geist medium; paper #F7F7F0; charcoal #141B19; lime #B7F36B; green #17653A.
96px safe area. Headlines 72–112px, supporting copy 30–40px, UI close-ups large enough to read.
Rounded product surfaces, restrained contact shadows, no fake people or vanity statistics.
Continuous frame-driven entrances, short transitions, still reading holds.

## Audio

Original synthesized background score and subtle interface cues. Narration always owns the mix.
