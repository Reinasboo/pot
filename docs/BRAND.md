# POT brand

**Good things, pooled.**

POT gives a group's shared plans a shared money plan. Speak to the person organizing the trip,
gift, or community idea. Lead with what the group makes possible, then explain the money rules.

## Identity

The pooling mark brings three individual contributions into one open vessel. Its outline keeps
the idea of a pot visible. The custom uppercase wordmark uses rounded strokes and an enclosed O.
Use the complete logo in navigation and the standalone mark for compact contexts. Preserve its
proportions and leave clear space at least as wide as the smallest circle around it.

Editable SVG assets: `apps/web/public/brand/pot-logo.svg` and `pot-mark.svg`.
The shared application logo is `apps/web/components/brand.tsx`; the favicon is `app/icon.svg`.
The social image font is a static medium instance derived from the installed Geist variable font;
its open font license is included alongside it in `public/brand/GEIST-LICENSE.txt`.

## Palette and typography

| Role                | Value     |
| ------------------- | --------- |
| Primary ink         | `#141B19` |
| Paper background    | `#F7F7F0` |
| Action / brand lime | `#B7F36B` |
| Green ink           | `#17653A` |
| Secondary text      | `#60695F` |
| Surface             | `#FFFFFF` |

Use charcoal text on lime, never small white text on lime. Lime highlights the main action and
one deliberate detail, not every surface. Geist is the interface typeface. Use regular for body
copy and medium/semibold for hierarchy, with tabular numbers for financial values.

## Voice

- Main hook: **Big plans. One shared pot.**
- Brand line: **Good things, pooled.**
- Primary action while payments are unavailable: **Plan your pot**.
- Closing invitation: **That “we should” idea? Give it a pot.**
- Write like the helpful person in the group, with short, concrete sentences.
- Prefer “money rules”, “chip in”, and “payout wallet” to unexplained contract terminology.
- State refund limitations alongside refund claims. Never imply refunds happen automatically.
- Show the current service status clearly. Do not present example goals as confirmed funding.
- Do not invent social proof, payment activity, audits, partners, or product capabilities.
